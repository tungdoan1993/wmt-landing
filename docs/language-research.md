# Language research — Farsi & Amharic (8 Nov 2026)

## Farsi (Iran) — terms Iranian prop-firm sites use, and what we chose

| Concept | Market term (searched) | On the page | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prop firm | پراپ فرم / شرکت پراپ | پراپ فرم (title, SEO) | Most-searched term ("بهترین پراپ برای ایرانیان") |
| Funded account | حساب فاند شده / حساب فاندد | حساب فاند شده مجازی | Standard term; "مجازی" keeps compliance |
| Get funded | فاند شدن | فاند شوید | Headline verb |
| 2-phase challenge | چالش دومرحله‌ای | چالش دومرحله‌ای | Standard |
| Instant funding | فاند فوری / حساب فوری | فاند فوری | Short, clear |
| Profit target | هدف سود / تارگت | هدف | "سود" (profit) avoided for compliance |
| Drawdown | دراداون روزانه / حداکثر دراداون | (not on the lean page) | For the bot / FAQ if needed |
| Profit split | تقسیم سود | سهم پاداش | Compliance: reward, not profit |
| Payout / withdrawal | برداشت / برداشت سود / درخواست برداشت | برداشت | Natural; "سود" dropped |
| Consistency rule | قانون ثبات | (bot / FAQ) | — |
| Scaling plan | طرح ارتقا / اسکیل آپ | — | — |
| Leverage | لوریج | — | — |
| Trader | تریدر / معامله‌گر | تریدر | Market uses the loanword |

Notes: Iranian articles stress payout speed, Tether (USDT) payments and discount codes ("کد تخفیف پراپ"). WMT's site says "USDC & stablecoins" — confirm USDT payouts before naming Tether on the page.

Sources: fxnx.com Farsi articles on one- vs two-step challenges and on withdrawals; search results from iranbroker.net, gadgetnews.net ("بهترین پراپ برای ایرانیان 1405"), eghtesadnews.com, khanesarmaye.com (several sites block automated reading; terms taken from titles/snippets).

Notes: Ethiopian traders learn mainly through Telegram groups and Amharic YouTube, pay through slow international bank transfers or crypto P2P, and test the payment cycle with the smallest account first — so the page leads with Telegram, the $45 entry and fast payouts. Prime trading hours (London/New York) fall at 11:00–24:00 EAT.
